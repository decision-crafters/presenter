# Sunrise

## Overview
A friendly, approachable brand for onboarding and educational decks: warm and
encouraging, never childish.

## Voice
Warm and plain-spoken. Address the audience as "you". Explain the why before the
how, and celebrate small wins.

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do use friendly, second-person phrasing.
- Do leave breathing room — never crowd a slide.
- Don't hide behind jargon; define a term the first time it appears.
