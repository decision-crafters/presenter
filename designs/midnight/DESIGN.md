# Midnight

## Overview
A confident, technical brand for developer-facing talks: a calm dark canvas, high
contrast, and a single bright accent doing the emphasis.

## Voice
Direct and precise. Short declarative sentences. Lead with the concrete example;
skip hype and marketing adjectives. Assume a smart, busy engineer.

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do keep lines short and let the accent color carry emphasis.
- Do favor a single clean diagram over a paragraph of text.
- Don't use exclamation marks or superlatives.
